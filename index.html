The School Shooting Trend

And their recent emergence in another country

Author: Traveler | August 26, 2026


These acts of gun violences against children in what was supposed to be a safe environment are, as unforunate as it is, nothing new.

We've seen and heard news of these before, most of which were from the United States.


Towards these past 3 months, we've been seeing a trend like this happening in schools in our country. The Philippines.

Nothing close to the level of the Columbine Shooting, but can easily snowball to match, or even surpass the fatalities of the Columbine Shooting.

Such examples of which are the Ateneo and Tacloban Incident. both of which were caused by Junior High School Perpetrators.


Why were these incidents of gun violence happening in the first place? Are gun laws slowly becoming loose like in the United States?

Did a chain-reaction happen that caused these shootings? Why did the prepetrators of both incidents do something like this in the first place?


As much as our moronic government would want you to think, it's not video games. they want shift blame to away from themselves as they indirectly caused this. Years of Poverty, Lack of and or Deliberate prevention of any action to improve the country's infrastructure (e.g; Flood Control), and Blatant Corruption, can cause a chain reaction that makes the students, or the aunts of the students, or even parents of the students to snap, and cause a shooting like this to happen.


In my opinion, it's more likely that the perpetrators were infuriated by their school, were constantly bullied, had no feasable way of accessing any mental health programs, thus letting their mental illness build up and cause something like this to happen rather than external means

like Video Games.


The question is, what can we do about it?


Now while i personally believe that adding better security around the school with their own firearms to actually fight back against the perpetrators is a great way to prevent something like this happening, it shouldn't be the only layer of protection we have to prevent something like a school shooting to happen.


We, the students can help. We can advocate for more or improved Mental Health Programs, we can address bullying and cyberbullying better, take school shooting jokes more seriously, and be nicer to our fellow students.


that or we could be forced to use only transparent bags. which would you prefer?
